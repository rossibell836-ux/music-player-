                    Spatial audio

                    8D uses real binaural spatialization to continuously rotate the sound around your head — front, side, back, side, and around again. 3D uses the same spatial audio technology but for a subtler sense of depth and position, gently drifting in distance and height rather than spinning. Both need headphones to work.
